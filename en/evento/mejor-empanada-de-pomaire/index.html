Pomaire and the empanada are inseparable. Every weekend, thousands of visitors head to the pottery town looking for that giant, clay-oven, crispy-crust empanada that became a legend of Chilean tourism. Now that love has its own celebration: the Best Empanada of Pomaire Contest.

Over a single day, local cooks, restaurants and family eateries compete with their finest creations. A people's jury — the visitors themselves — and a panel of food experts decide who takes home the title of the year.

📅DateSaturday, September 5, 2026
🕚Start11:30 AM · Pomaire Main Square
📍VenuePomaire Main Square
🎟️EntryFree for the public

What you'll find

  • Empanada tastings from the participating eateries, from the classic beef "pino" to seafood, cheese and surprise creations.
  • Live clay-oven demonstration, the traditional technique behind Pomaire's unique flavor.
  • Public voting to pick the favorite empanada of the year.
  • Live music and a family atmosphere in the heart of the town.
  • Clay craft fair open all day long.
