Where to Stay in Pomaire: Cabins, Guesthouses and Rural Lodging

Last updated: September 2026 | Reading time: 6 minutes

Most people visit Pomaire as a day trip from Santiago, but staying a night is a great option if you want to enjoy the village at a relaxed pace: touring the clay workshops calmly, having a long lunch and exploring the rural surroundings of Melipilla the next day. In this guide we tell you what types of lodging there are, reference prices and tips for booking in 2026.

Types of lodging in Pomaire

Pomaire is a small village, so the lodging on offer is cosy and family-run, with no large hotels. These are the most common types:

  • Cabins: The most popular option, ideal for couples or families. Many are on small plots a few blocks from the square, with an equipped kitchen and a quiet countryside setting.
  • Bed & Breakfast: Lodging in houses with breakfast included, with a warm welcome and rustic decor.
  • Guesthouses and hostels: Simple, affordable options within the village's tourist area.
  • Rural lodging / plots: For those seeking nature, silence and a more rural experience in the surroundings.

When it's worth staying overnight

  • A relaxed weekend: Arriving on Saturday afternoon and sleeping over lets you enjoy Sunday early, before the crowds.
  • An extended route: If you want to combine Pomaire with the Melipilla wine route or the surroundings, one night gives you plenty of time.
  • Events and festivals: During folk festivals or the Fiestas Patrias, staying over avoids the drive back in traffic.

Tips for booking lodging in Pomaire

  1. Book in advance on weekends and holidays: availability is limited.
  2. Confirm the location: some cabins are on plots on the outskirts; check the distance to the square if you don't have a car.
  3. Ask what's included: breakfast, equipped kitchen, parking, heating in winter.
  4. Check payment options: some small lodgings prefer bank transfer or cash.
  5. Bring warm clothes in winter: country nights in Melipilla are cold.

Frequently asked questions
