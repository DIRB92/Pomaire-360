Frequently Asked Questions about Pomaire

Last updated: September 2026

We have gathered the most common questions from those planning a visit to Pomaire, the most famous pottery village in Chile. Here you will find quick answers about how to get there, opening hours, prices, parking and much more.

How do I get to Pomaire from Santiago?

By car, take the Autopista del Sol (Route 78) heading west and take the exit at km 50 towards Pomaire; the trip takes about 50 minutes. By public transport, take a bus to Melipilla from the Terminal San Borja (Estación Central metro) and from Melipilla a colectivo or local bus to Pomaire. More details in our guide on how to get to Pomaire.

How long does it take to get to Pomaire from Santiago?

By car, about 50 minutes without traffic. On weekend mornings the trip can rise to 1 hour and 15 minutes because of congestion on the motorway.

Is Pomaire open on Mondays?

The village is always open, but on Mondays many workshops and restaurants close or reduce their hours. The best days to visit with everything open are Tuesday to Sunday.

How much does it cost to visit Pomaire?

Walking around the village and entering the workshops is free. A half-day outing costs approximately between $20,000 and $50,000 CLP per person, including lunch, transport and a craft souvenir.

Where can I park in Pomaire?

There is free parking on the main street and at the northern entrance of the village, as well as private car parks at some restaurants. On weekends it fills up before 11:00, so it is best to arrive early. See more at parking in Pomaire.

How much time do I need to visit Pomaire?

Half a day (4 to 5 hours) is enough to walk around the workshops, have lunch and shop for crafts. If you want to take it slowly or combine it with the Melipilla Wine Route, you can dedicate the whole day. See our one-day itinerary.

Is Pomaire suitable for children?

Yes, it is a very family-friendly destination. Children enjoy watching the artisans work the clay, some workshops offer classes to make pieces, and there are attractions such as the giant piggy bank. Check our guide to Pomaire with kids.

How much does the giant empanada of Pomaire cost?

The giant empanada, which weighs close to one kilo, costs approximately between $6,000 and $7,000 CLP. A regular baked empanada costs between $3,500 and $5,000 CLP. See the guide to where to eat in Pomaire.

What should I buy in Pomaire?

The most typical thing is clay pottery: piggy banks (from $2,000 CLP), pots and dishes for cooking, tableware, planters and signature decorative pieces. Buying directly from the workshops supports the artisan and is usually cheaper.

Can I pay by card in Pomaire?

The large restaurants accept cards, but many workshops and small shops only take cash. It is recommended to bring cash for craft purchases.

What is the best time to visit Pomaire?

Pomaire can be visited all year round. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) offer pleasant weather and fewer people. In summer there is more atmosphere but more heat and crowds.

Do I have to pay to see the pottery workshops?

No, entering the workshops and watching the artisans work is free. You only pay if you take a pottery class (between $10,000 and $20,000 CLP) or if you buy a piece.
