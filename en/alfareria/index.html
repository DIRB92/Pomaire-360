Pottery — the heart of Pomaire

Workshops, shopping and the craft tradition that defines this village

🏺
Crafts · Main street

Street of the potters

Rafael Morandé is the main street, full of workshops and shops where artisans work and sell live. Clay pieces: jugs, pots, jars and more.

📍 View on map
🎨
Open workshop

Watch potters at work

Many artisans work with their wheels and kilns in plain view. You can see the whole process: from raw clay to the finished piece.

🛍️
Buying tips

Buy crafts responsibly

Prefer buying directly from the artisan. Avoid haggling aggressively — each piece carries work and family tradition behind it.

🏆
Heritage

Intangible heritage of Chile

Pomaire's pottery is recognized as cultural heritage. Techniques handed down from generation to generation for centuries.
