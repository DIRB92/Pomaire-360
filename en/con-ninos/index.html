Pomaire with Kids: The Perfect Family Outing near Santiago

Last updated: September 2026 | Reading time: 6 minutes

Pomaire is one of the best family outings near Santiago. Just 50 minutes from the capital, it offers children an entertaining and educational experience: watching the artisans mould the clay, getting their hands into the mud, meeting the giant piggy bank and eating well in a calm, open-air atmosphere. Here we tell you how to make the most of it as a family.

Activities for children in Pomaire

  • A clay workshop to make their own piece: Several workshops offer classes where children mould their own little piggy bank or vessel. It is the star activity: fun, sensory and with a keepsake to take home.
  • Watching the artisans work: Children are fascinated by seeing how a lump of clay becomes a pot or figure on the wheel. Entering the workshops is free.
  • The World's Largest Clay Piggy Bank: A giant clay sculpture, perfect for family photos and to amaze the little ones.
  • A walk around the square: An open, safe space for children to walk while the adults browse the crafts.
  • Buying a piggy bank: An affordable keepsake (from $2,000 CLP) that children love and teaches them to save.
Tip: Book the pottery class for the morning, when children are fresher and full of energy. Ask at the workshop about the recommended minimum age.

Where to eat with kids

Many restaurants in Pomaire have a patio or terrace and plenty of space, which makes them comfortable for going with children. The portions are generous, so a giant empanada can be enough to share among several people. There are also simple options that tend to please the youngest, such as cheese empanadas or humitas.

Practical tips for going as a family

  1. Flat streets, on foot: The village is explored on foot along flat streets, comfortable for a stroller (although the surface can be uneven in some stretches).
  2. Arrive early: Especially on weekends, to park nearby and avoid walking a lot with tired children.
  3. Bring water and sun protection: In summer the sun is strong and there is little shade on the main street.
  4. Clothes that can get dirty: If they are going to do a clay workshop, the mud stains! Bring a change of clothes or an apron.
  5. Cash on hand: For the children's quick purchases at the stalls.

Recommended by age

  • Babies and under 3: The walk and the square work well; the clay workshop is better left for later.
  • 4 to 10 years: The ideal age for the pottery workshop and to enjoy the giant piggy bank and the shops.
  • Pre-teens and teenagers: They enjoy creating more elaborate pieces and exploring the village with more independence.
Full-day idea: Combine Pomaire with a long lunch and, if there is energy left, a short stroll around the rural surroundings of Melipilla. A complete family plan without going far from Santiago.
