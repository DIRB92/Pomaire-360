2. Purpose of this policy

This policy describes how Pomaire 360 collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal data of users of the website www.pomaire360.cl and its subdomains, in compliance with:

  • Law No. 19.628 on Protection of Private Life (in force until Dec 2026).
  • Law No. 21.719 regulating the Protection and Processing of Personal Data and creating the Personal Data Protection Agency (full enforcement: December 1, 2026).
  • Law No. 21.663 Cybersecurity Framework, where applicable.

6. Your rights (ARCO + Portability)

Under Chilean law, as a personal data subject you have the right to:

Right Description
AccessKnow what personal data we hold about you and obtain a copy.
RectificationCorrect inaccurate or incomplete data.
Cancellation (Deletion)Request the deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary.
OppositionObject to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
PortabilityReceive your data in a structured and commonly used format.

6.1 How to exercise your rights

  1. Send an email to contacto@pomaire360.cl with the subject line "Exercise of ARCO rights".
  2. Include your full name and the right you wish to exercise.
  3. To verify your identity, we will ask for your name and email address associated with your previous request (suggestions form).

6.2 Response deadlines

  • Maximum deadline: 15 business days from receipt of your request.
  • If the request is complex, we may extend the deadline by an additional 15 business days, notifying you of the reason.
  • Exercising these rights is free of charge.

7. Security measures

We implement the following technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: Mandatory HTTPS with HSTS preload (TLS certificate).
  • Content Security Policy (CSP): Protection against malicious script injection (XSS).
  • Security headers: X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy.
  • Data sanitization: All data input is sanitized before use to prevent attacks.
  • Minimization: We only collect the data strictly necessary for each purpose.
  • Anonymization: IPs are anonymized before being processed by Google Analytics.
  • Local storage: Preferences are stored on your device (localStorage), without transmission to servers.

8. Minors' data

Pomaire 360 is not directed at minors under 14 years of age and does not intentionally collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided personal data through our form, contact us at contacto@pomaire360.cl for immediate deletion.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal changes or changes in our practices. In case of substantial modifications:

  • We will post a visible notice on the site for at least 7 days.
  • We will update the "last revised" date at the bottom of this page.
  • If the change affects consent-based processing, we will request new consent.

10. Right to complaint

If you believe that the processing of your data violates your rights, you may file a complaint with:

  • Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (created by Law 21.719, operational from December 2026).
  • Ordinary courts of justice in accordance with article 23 of Law 19.628 (procedure in force until the Agency is fully operational).

11. Applicable law and jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Chile. For any dispute, the parties submit to the ordinary courts of justice with jurisdiction in the commune of Melipilla, Metropolitan Region.
