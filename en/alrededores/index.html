Around Pomaire

Los Chiñihues and nearby areas worth exploring

🌾
Rural area · Nearby

Los Chiñihues

A rural locality very close to Pomaire. Coastal dryland landscape with vineyards and ravines. Ideal for those seeking peace and nature away from the tourist center.

📍 View on map
🍇
Rural routes

Dryland roads and wines

The area around Pomaire is a traditional wine-growing zone. Small family producers of chicha and wine in Los Chiñihues and nearby roads.

🏔️
Nature

Hills and ravines

The hills surrounding Pomaire offer views over the Melipilla valley. There are informal trails accessible from the road linking the town with Los Chiñihues.

🚗
Access

How to get there from Santiago

Autopista del Sol (Route 78) toward Melipilla. Exit at km 51 with signage to Pomaire. About 50 min from Santiago by car.

📍 How to get there
🚌
Public transport

Buses from Santiago/Melipilla

There are buses from Terminal San Borja (Santiago) stopping in Melipilla. From Melipilla, rural minibuses run to Pomaire regularly.

📍 Melipilla terminal
📡
Connectivity

Mobile signal and WiFi

Cell signal in Pomaire is reasonable downtown, but can be weak in Los Chiñihues and rural areas. Some restaurants offer WiFi.
