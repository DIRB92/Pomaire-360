Fiestas Patrias in Pomaire: September 18 in the Pottery Village

Last updated: September 2026 | Reading time: 6 minutes

The Fiestas Patrias —Chile's national holidays, popularly known as "el Dieciocho" (the Eighteenth)— are the country's most important celebration, and Pomaire is one of the favourite spots to experience them near Santiago. Just 50 minutes from the capital, this pottery village offers exactly what people look for on September 18: clay-oven empanadas, artisanal chicha, hearty country cuisine and a warm, folk (huaso) atmosphere. In this guide we tell you what you will find and how to make the most of your visit while avoiding traffic and crowds.

The Dieciocho 2026: the Fiestas Patrias are celebrated on September 18 and 19. These are very busy dates in Pomaire, so plan ahead.

Why Pomaire is ideal for September 18

Pomaire brings together the essence of the Chilean national holidays in one place:

  • Authentic traditional food: Clay-oven empanadas and country cuisine served in clay pots, the heart of the Fiestas Patrias table.
  • Folk, traditional atmosphere: Streets full of crafts, music and the countryside spirit of central Chile.
  • Close to Santiago: Ideal for a day trip without travelling far on a long weekend.
  • A family plan: An outing for all ages, combining food, crafts and a stroll.

What to eat in Pomaire during Fiestas Patrias

  • Clay-oven empanada: The star of the Dieciocho, including Pomaire's famous giant empanada.
  • Cazuela and pastel de choclo: Served in clay pots and dishes, comforting country cooking.
  • Arrollado huaso and pork cuts: For lovers of hearty food.
  • Artisanal chicha: Grape or apple, the typical drink of the Dieciocho.

How to avoid traffic and queues

Pomaire is one of the busiest destinations during the Dieciocho, and the Autopista del Sol (Route 78) gets quite congested on these dates. To make your visit comfortable:

  1. Leave early: Before 9:00 AM to avoid traffic on the way and to find parking.
  2. Have lunch off-peak: Before 12:30 or after 15:00 to avoid waits at the restaurants.
  3. Plan your return: Avoid heading back to Santiago at the busiest return time; wait a bit or leave earlier.
  4. Bring cash: With more people, checkout queues are longer; cash speeds things up at small venues.

Tips for your Dieciocho visit

  • Consider staying overnight: If the long weekend allows it, staying over avoids the drive back in traffic. See where to stay in Pomaire.
  • Take the chance to buy crafts: A clay piggy bank is the perfect souvenir. See what to buy in Pomaire.
  • Comfortable clothes and sun protection: September can have strong midday sun.
  • Patience: It's a holiday and there are lots of people; taking it easy is part of enjoying the Dieciocho.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth going to Pomaire for September 18?

Yes, it's a classic Dieciocho outing near Santiago thanks to its traditional food and folk atmosphere. Just keep in mind that it's very busy: it's best to arrive early.

Is Pomaire open during Fiestas Patrias?

Yes, on September 18 and 19 the village is usually very active, with most restaurants and several workshops open. It is one of the busiest dates of the year.
