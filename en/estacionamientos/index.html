Parking

Thousands of visitors arrive every weekend — plan where to park your car

🅿️
Main · North entrance

Pomaire Entrance Parking

Rafael Morandé street, in front of the main access. The largest in town. On weekends it can fill up before 11:00.

📍 View on map
🚗
Side · Artisans street

18 de Septiembre Street Parking

Roadside spaces on 18 de Septiembre street, next to the town center. An option if the north entrance is full.

📍 View on map
🚌
Buses and minibuses

Tourist bus zone

Area set aside for tour buses and vans. Ask the guard at the north entrance.

📍 View area
💡
Tip

Parking tips

Arriving before 10:30 on weekends helps you avoid the crowds. Don't block neighbors' driveways or rural roads.
