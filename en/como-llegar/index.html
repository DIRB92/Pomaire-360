How to Get to Pomaire from Santiago

Last updated: September 2026 | Reading time: 6 minutes

Pomaire is just 50 kilometres west of Santiago, in the district of Melipilla (Metropolitan Region), which makes it one of the most accessible day trips from the capital. Here we explain all the ways to get there, with times, costs and practical tips updated to 2026.

How to get there by car

The most comfortable and fastest way to reach Pomaire is by car via the Autopista del Sol (Route 78), the road that connects Santiago with San Antonio.

  1. From Santiago, get on the Autopista del Sol (Route 78) heading west (towards San Antonio).
  2. Continue along the motorway and take the exit to Pomaire, signposted at approximately km 50.
  3. Follow the signs to the centre of the village, where the workshops, restaurants and shops are concentrated.
  4. Park on the main street or at the northern entrance of the village.

The trip from central Santiago takes approximately 50 minutes without traffic. The motorway is a toll road (~$3,800 CLP each way).

Tip: If you go on a weekend, leave before 9:00 AM. Between 9:00 and 11:00 the motorway gets congested and the trip can take up to 1 hour and 15 minutes.

How to get there by bus / public transport

Getting there by public transport requires a connection, but it is entirely feasible and affordable:

  1. Take an intercity bus to Melipilla at the Terminal San Borja, located next to the Estación Central metro. The fare is around $2,500 CLP.
  2. On arriving in Melipilla, take a shared taxi (colectivo) or local bus to Pomaire (~15 minutes, ~$800 CLP). They run frequently during the day.
  3. Some routes from Melipilla go directly through Pomaire: ask the driver whether the route includes the stop in the village.
Tip: The last return from Melipilla to Santiago is usually in the late afternoon. Check the return times beforehand so you don't get stranded, especially in winter when it gets dark earlier.

How to get there on an organised tour

If you prefer not to drive or connect transport, several tour agencies in Santiago offer half-day tours to Pomaire that include round-trip transport, a guide and free time to have lunch and shop. Prices range from $25,000 to $45,000 CLP per person, depending on the agency and what is included.

Parking when you arrive

Pomaire is a small village that is best explored on foot. When you arrive by car you can park at:

  • Main street: Free on-street parking, more limited on weekends.
  • Northern entrance of the village: A large lot with space for cars and buses.
  • Private car parks: Some restaurants offer parking for their customers.

See more details in our guide to parking in Pomaire.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a direct bus from Santiago to Pomaire?

There is usually no direct bus. The common way is to take a bus to Melipilla from the Terminal San Borja and from there a colectivo or local bus to Pomaire (about 15 minutes).

How much does it cost to get there by public transport?

Around $5,000 to $6,600 CLP round trip per person, adding the bus to Melipilla and the local colectivo.
