🐷 A must-see attraction in Pomaire

The world's largest clay piggy bank

Created by César Silva Rojo

See the Story How to Visit

César Silva Rojo

Master Potter · Pomaire, Chile

A master potter from Pomaire and creator of the world's largest clay piggy bank. With years of craft shaping Pomaire's clay, he turned an impossible idea into a unique work that is now an attraction of the town.

The story of an impossible idea

  1. 💡

    The idea

    It all starts with a dream: to create the world's largest clay piggy bank, taking Pomaire's tradition to a scale never seen before.

  2. ✏️

    The design

    Sketches and calculations to define the proportions, structure and strength of a giant clay piece.

  3. 🤲

    The modeling

    Handwork: shaping the clay with the technique inherited from generations of Pomaire potters.

  4. 🌬️

    The drying

    Days of patience so the clay slowly loses moisture and avoids cracks in a work of this size.

  5. 🔥

    The firing

    The most delicate step: the kiln's fire hardens the clay and gives it its final terracotta color.

  6. 🏆

    The finished work

    The result: the world's largest clay piggy bank, the pride of Pomaire and an attraction for its visitors.
