Traditional Food

Traditional flavours served in clay pots

🥘
Signature dish

Clay-pot cazuela

Pomaire's most iconic dish. Cazuelas, empanadas and pastel de choclo served in clay pots made in the town itself.

🥟
Classic

Giant empanadas

Pomaire is famous for its huge empanadas. There are empanada shops all along the main street.

🍷
Drinks

Chicha and traditional drinks

Apple and grape chicha, mote con huesillo and artisanal drinks. Perfect to accompany the local food.

🏪
Where to eat

Restaurants and eateries

Numerous restaurants on the main street and its side streets. On weekends there may be a wait. We recommend arriving before noon.
