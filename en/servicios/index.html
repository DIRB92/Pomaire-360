Shops & Everyday Services

The basics for living or spending the day in Pomaire

🏪
Grocery · Center

Shops on the main street

Several grocery shops and minimarkets on and around Rafael Morandé. They sell water, snacks and basics for visitors.

📍 View area
🔧
Mechanic · Road

Mechanic / car workshop

There are small workshops along the access road and on the way to Melipilla. For serious breakdowns the nearest help is in Melipilla city.

📍 Find workshops
⛽
Fuel

Gas stations

The nearest gas station is on the road to Melipilla. We recommend arriving with a full tank if you come from Santiago.

📍 View on map
🏧
ATM · Cash

ATMs

There is an ATM inside the town at Roberto Bravo 445 street. Many shops accept cards.

Post / Parcels

Correos Chile

The nearest post office is in Melipilla city. Arrange shipments from there.
