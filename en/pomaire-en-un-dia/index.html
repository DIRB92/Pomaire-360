Pomaire in One Day: Full Itinerary from Santiago

Last updated: September 2026 | Reading time: 7 minutes

Pomaire is the perfect day trip from Santiago: culture, crafts and food just 50 minutes from the capital. With this hour-by-hour itinerary you will make the most of your visit to the most famous pottery village in Chile, without leaving anything important out.

In this guide

  • Hour-by-hour itinerary
  • Summary of the day

Hour-by-hour itinerary

  • 09:00Departure from Santiago. Take the Autopista del Sol (Route 78) early to avoid weekend congestion. See how to get to Pomaire.
  • 10:00Arrival and coffee. Park at the entrance of the village and start with a coffee or sopaipillas while the village wakes up. By this time the workshops are already opening.
  • 10:30Tour of the clay workshops. Walk the main street and go into the workshops to see the artisans work the clay live. Ideal for taking photos and choosing what you will buy later.
  • 12:00Pottery class (optional). Several workshops offer 1-hour experiences where you create your own piece. An ideal activity to do calmly before lunch.
  • 13:00Traditional lunch. Try the famous giant baked empanada and a cazuela served in a clay pot. See where to eat in Pomaire.
  • 14:30The World's Largest Clay Piggy Bank. Visit the giant clay sculpture, one of Pomaire's most photogenic icons.
  • 15:00Pomaire main square. Stroll around the main square and the San Antonio de Padua church, with a craft fair and a family atmosphere.
  • 15:30Craft shopping. Now yes, buy what you chose in the morning: piggy banks, pots, tableware or decorative pieces. Buying at the end avoids carrying fragile clay all day.
  • 16:30Return or extension. Head back to Santiago calmly, or extend the day by visiting the nearby Melipilla Wine Route.
Tip: If you go on a weekday, everything will be quieter and you won't need to get up as early. On weekends, however, it is best to leave before 9:00 to find parking and avoid queues at the restaurants.

Summary of the day

  • Morning: Clay workshops + optional pottery class.
  • Midday: Traditional lunch with a giant empanada.
  • Afternoon: Giant piggy bank, main square and craft shopping.
  • Optional extension: Melipilla Wine Route.

Estimated budget per person

  • Transport (car or bus): ~$5,000 – $7,600 CLP
  • Full lunch: $8,000 – $15,000 CLP
  • Pottery class (optional): $10,000 – $20,000 CLP
  • Craft souvenir: $2,000 – $15,000 CLP
  • Estimated total: $20,000 – $50,000 CLP

Tips to make the most of the day

  1. Arrive early on weekends to park without problems.
  2. Bring cash: many workshops only take cash.
  3. Wear comfortable shoes: the tour is on foot along uneven streets.
  4. Buy at the end of the tour so you don't carry fragile pieces around.
  5. Ask about shipping if you buy large pieces: several workshops deliver to Santiago.
